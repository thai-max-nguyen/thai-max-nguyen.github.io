Shipping products

Five launches, newest first.

Zalopay · Buy Now Pay Later · Coaching a junior product manager · VnEconomy on Zalopay and CIMB ↗

Buy Now Pay Later, end to end, across lenders

Credit inside the wallet: a bank or finance company lends, Zalopay runs the product. I own it with a junior product manager I coach, and work with the lender and our risk, legal and compliance teams.

Zalopay · Sign-up · VnExpress on the VNeID launch ↗

Sign-up success quadrupled with the national digital ID

I owned sign-up success, the step in front of every revenue service. I added reading the ID card’s chip with the phone (NFC), sign-up through VNeID, the national digital ID app run by Vietnam’s Ministry of Public Security, a way to resume where people dropped off, and AI auto-capture of the ID card.

Also in identity: closing an account and paying out what is left in it, locking and unlocking, recycled phone numbers, and PIN reset and change.

Zalopay · Merchants · Sao Khuê Award 2025 ↗

The first e-commerce checkout in Vietnam on VietQR, the national QR standard

I brought VietQR to Lazada checkout. Any banking app or e-wallet in the country can scan it, and the amount, recipient and order reference arrive pre-filled, so the buyer only confirms.

Then payment on delivery

I owned Zalopay payment on delivery and led the Lazada Express and BEST Express integrations. Couriers collect cash-on-delivery orders with a Zalopay QR inside their own delivery app. Each order gets its own code for the exact amount, any bank or e-wallet app can scan it, and the money goes straight to the company’s account. Partners include Ahamove, Lazada Express and BEST Express, which rolled it out across its network in 63 provinces.

Ride-hailing: Xanh SM and VNPAY Taxi

I was the product manager for both integrations. For Xanh SM, the electric taxi service, I connected Zalopay to the Xanh SM app and brought its mini app into Zalopay. Zalopay holds the estimated fare at booking and charges the final fare at drop-off (pre-authorisation). For VNPAY Taxi, I brought its mini app into Zalopay: people link their wallet once, confirm with Face ID, then book and pay without leaving Zalopay.

Improving what was already live

I owned the Zalopay payment integrations with Google Play, Grab and Gojek after launch, working on their core metrics: payment success rate and conversion at checkout.

Zalopay · Apple payments · Báo Đầu tư on the App Store launch ↗

51% of users who linked Zalopay went on to pay, 3.4x the regional benchmark

Product lead for Zalopay as a tokenised payment method across the App Store, Apple Music and Apple TV. I worked through Apple’s payment standards with their team in Seoul.

Then growth: a web flow and Apple Hub

People without the Zalopay app link through a web flow I built. It brings in about 40% of new Apple payers. I also built Apple Hub, a mini app inside Zalopay that walks people through linking their Apple Account, choosing the funding source and managing the link, then sends them to the App Store to finish.

Lazada · Bank partnerships · Best Employee Award 2023 · VnExpress on the LazCard launch ↗

The first co-branded card across all Lazada markets

I led LazCard with VIB, with sign-up and identity checks inside the Lazada app, and brought VPBank on as a payments partner.

What the bank deals did: card orders got cancelled less and settled faster, banks funded promotions, and card payers came back more often.

Shipping under regulation

  • Central bank Circular 40 · e-wallet identity

    Before anyone pays from the wallet, five checks

    1. Aged 16 or overread from the ID card; under 16 is blocked
    2. Identity verified (eKYC)with a chip ID card, redone when the details expire
    3. Chip ID card checked by NFCmatched with Ministry of Public Security records, or synced from VNeID
    4. A linked bank account in their own namesame ID and phone number as the Zalopay account
    5. Anything missing: the payment pausesthe app opens only the step still missing
